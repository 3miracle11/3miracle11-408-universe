# 必考词 Unit 09: The Immense Challenge of Accommodating Immigrants in a Democratic Society

中文标题：民主社会中容纳移民的巨大挑战

## English Article

In recent years, the **government** has faced an **immense** challenge: how to **accommodate** a growing number of **immigrants** while maintaining social harmony and **health** standards. This issue has become even more **imminent** as global migration increases, and the **demographic** landscape shifts dramatically. The **federal** and local authorities must **govern** effectively to **accomplish** this goal.

One **eminent** expert on immigration policy recently gave a **grand** speech highlighting the **features** of successful integration. She emphasized the importance of creating **accommodation** that not only provide shelter but also foster a sense of belonging. "We must not **deny** the **emotions** and **fear** that immigrants often experience," she said. "Instead, we should **accompany** them on their journey to becoming **healthy**, contributing members of society, and help them **immerse** themselves fully in their new environment."

The challenge is **likely** to grow as **billions** of people worldwide seek better opportunities. The **government** must **account** for the **dependent** populations that arise, including children and the elderly. This requires a **gradual** approach, balancing immediate needs with long-term sustainability and ensuring adequate **accommodation** and social services.

Economic factors also play a crucial role. The demand for **goods** and services increases, and immigrants often **feed** the labor market with their skills. However, some critics express **bias** and **fearful** attitudes, which can lead to **denial** of immigrants' rights and contributions. To combat this, public education campaigns aim to **highlight** positive stories and reduce prejudice, demonstrating the **charm** and value immigrants bring to society.

In times of crisis, such as an **emergency** health outbreak, the **immune** system of the community is tested. Immigrants, like all residents, must have access to healthcare to **heal** and recover. The **department** of health works tirelessly to ensure that no one is left behind.

The **likelihood** of successful integration improves when immigrants feel welcomed and supported. Programs that offer language training and cultural orientation help newcomers **graduate** from isolation to active participation. Feedback from these programs is essential to refine policies and practices. Likewise, immigrants should not **hesitate** to seek help and **grant** themselves the opportunity to thrive.

Some immigrants arrive with a **bias** against their new home due to past experiences, but many **emerge** with a new sense of identity and purpose. Their **self**-confidence grows as they **bid** farewell to old fears and **chase** new dreams. It is important to **denote** that a **fertile** environment for growth and acceptance can reduce feelings of being **depressed** or **selfish**.

The **bill** for these initiatives is often high, but the **government** sees it as a worthwhile investment. The **governor** of one state remarked, "Our **democratic** society thrives when all voices are heard and respected."

In conclusion, the **acclaim** for successful immigrant integration is well-deserved. It requires effort from all sectors of society, a commitment to fairness, and a recognition that we all **depend** on each other as part of a larger human family. As the world continues to change, so must our approaches to welcoming those who seek a better life.

## 中文译文

近年来，政府面临着一项巨大的挑战：如何在保持社会和谐与健康标准的同时，容纳日益增长的移民数量。随着全球移民的增加和人口结构的剧烈变化，这一问题变得更加迫切。联邦和地方当局必须有效治理，以完成这一目标。

一位著名的移民政策专家最近发表了一场盛大的演讲，强调了成功融合的特点。她强调创建不仅提供住所而且促进归属感的住宿设施的重要性。“我们不能否认移民常常经历的情感和恐惧，”她说，“相反，我们应该陪伴他们踏上成为健康、有贡献的社会成员的旅程，并帮助他们完全沉浸于新的环境中。”

随着全球数十亿人寻求更好的机会，这一挑战可能会加剧。政府必须考虑由此产生的依赖人口，包括儿童和老年人。这需要一种渐进的方法，平衡眼前需求与长期可持续性，确保充足的住宿和社会服务。

经济因素也起着关键作用。对商品和服务的需求增加，移民常常用他们的技能滋养劳动力市场。然而，一些批评者表现出偏见和恐惧的态度，这可能导致对移民权利和贡献的否认。为此，公共教育运动旨在突出积极的故事，减少偏见，展示移民为社会带来的魅力和价值。

在危机时期，如紧急的健康爆发，社区的免疫系统受到考验。移民和所有居民一样，必须获得医疗服务以治愈和康复。卫生部门不懈努力，确保没有人被遗忘。

当移民感到受欢迎和支持时，成功融合的可能性提高。提供语言培训和文化指导的项目帮助新移民从孤立中毕业，积极参与社会。这些项目的反馈对于完善政策和实践至关重要。同样，移民不应犹豫寻求帮助，并给予自己茁壮成长的机会。

一些移民由于过去的经历对新家园抱有偏见，但许多人带着新的身份感和目标出现。他们的自信心随着告别旧有的恐惧、追逐新梦想而增长。值得指出的是，肥沃的成长和接纳环境可以减少抑郁或自私的情绪。

这些举措的账单通常很高，但政府认为这是值得的投资。一位州长评论说：“我们的民主社会在所有声音被听见和尊重时蓬勃发展。”

总之，对成功移民融合的赞誉实至名归。这需要社会各界的努力、对公平的承诺以及认识到我们彼此依赖，都是更大人类大家庭的一部分。随着世界不断变化，我们欢迎寻求更好生活者的方法也必须随之改变。
