# 基础词 Unit 03: The Kingdom of Grace: A Tale of Civilization and Kindness

中文标题：恩典王国：文明与善良的故事

## English Article

In a distant **kingdom**, nestled between dense forests and towering plateaus, a unique **civilization** thrived. This kingdom was known not only for its **golden** fields and **gold** mines but also for its **kindness** and **grace**. The people lived **alongside** nature, respecting every living **being** from the smallest **goat** to the tallest tree.

The **kingdom** was ruled by a wise **mayor**, who believed in the power of **alliance** and **allegiance** among the citizens. Unlike the old **feudal** systems where rulers demanded absolute control, this leader encouraged a **fellowship** where every **citizen** had a voice. The **mayor** often spoke about the importance of **alleviating** suffering and promoting peace.

One day, a group of **refugees** arrived at the kingdom’s borders. They had fled from a neighboring land torn apart by war and **civil** unrest. The **mayor** welcomed them with open arms, providing shelter and a chance to rebuild their lives. The refugees were grateful and quickly became part of the community, sharing their skills and stories.

Among the newcomers was a young woman who carried a small **kit** containing an **alphabet** book and some basic tools. She taught children how to read and write, helping to **induce** a love for learning. Her efforts helped the kingdom’s **network** of schools grow stronger, connecting people through knowledge.

The kingdom’s economy was diverse. Farmers worked the **mat**-covered fields, while artisans crafted goods from **plastic** and **plaster**. Traders traveled in a **circuit** around the kingdom, exchanging goods and news. The **density** of the population was moderate, allowing for a balance between urban and rural life.

In the evenings, people would gather in circular plazas, where musicians played and dancers performed with **graceful** movements. The atmosphere was **gracious**, filled with laughter and joy. Children played games that involved throwing a **net** or **matching** shapes in a **circle**.

Despite the peaceful life, challenges arose. Sometimes, the **mayor** had to **denounce** corruption or **refuse** unfair demands. The people learned to **refrain** from violence and instead resolve conflicts through dialogue. When illness struck, such as a sudden **fever**, the community came together to care for the sick, using remedies made from herbs and **optical** aids to monitor health.

The kingdom also had a unique tradition of honoring the **female** members of society, celebrating their **feminine** strength and wisdom. Women often led community projects and were respected as equals. This progressive attitude helped the kingdom maintain **neutrality** in regional disputes, avoiding unnecessary wars.

Every year, the kingdom held a festival on the **plateau**, where people would **fetch** water from the springs and share meals on large **plates**. The event symbolized unity and the **allowance** of differences among people. Songs about the **heaven** above and the earth below were sung, reminding everyone of their connection to the world.

The kingdom’s artisans also specialized in creating **alloys** of metals, combining strength and beauty. Their crafts were sought after far and wide, bringing wealth and recognition. The **heavy** work of mining and forging was balanced by moments of leisure and play.

In times of hardship, the community would **beg** for help from neighboring lands or from within their own network. But they never lost hope or their commitment to kindness. The **refusal** to give up was a testament to their spirit.

This story of the **kingdom** is a reminder that civilization is not just about power or wealth but about the bonds we build, the respect we show, and the grace with which we live together. It teaches us that even in a world full of challenges, kindness and cooperation can create a lasting legacy.

## 中文译文

在一个遥远的**王国**，坐落在茂密的森林和高耸的高原之间，一个独特的**文明**蓬勃发展。这个王国不仅以其**金色**的田野和**黄金**矿藏闻名，还以其**善良**和**恩典**著称。人们与大自然**并肩**生活，尊重从最小的**山羊**到最高的树木的每一个生命。

这个**王国**由一位智慧的**市长**统治，他相信公民之间的**联盟**和**效忠**的力量。不同于旧时要求绝对控制的**封建**制度，这位领导者鼓励建立一个每个**公民**都有发言权的**团体**。**市长**经常谈论减轻痛苦和促进和平的重要性。

有一天，一群**难民**抵达了王国的边境。他们逃离了一个因战争和**内战**动荡的邻国。**市长**张开双臂欢迎他们，提供庇护和重建生活的机会。难民们心怀感激，很快成为社区的一部分，分享他们的技能和故事。

新来者中有一位年轻女子，携带着一个小**工具包**，里面有一本**字母表**书和一些基本工具。她教孩子们识字，帮助激发对学习的热爱。她的努力使王国的学校**网络**更加坚固，通过知识连接人们。

王国的经济多样化。农民耕种覆盖着**草席**的田地，工匠用**塑料**和**石膏**制作商品。商人们绕着王国进行**循环**贸易，交换商品和新闻。人口的**密度**适中，城市和乡村生活保持平衡。

傍晚时分，人们会聚集在圆形广场，音乐家演奏，舞者以**优雅**的动作表演。气氛**亲切**，充满欢笑和喜悦。孩子们玩耍，游戏包括投掷**网**和匹配**圆圈**中的形状。

尽管生活平静，但挑战依然存在。有时，**市长**必须**谴责**腐败或**拒绝**不公正的要求。人们学会了**克制**暴力，通过对话解决冲突。当疾病来袭，比如突发的**发烧**，社区会团结起来照顾病人，使用草药和**光学**辅助设备监测健康。

王国还有尊重**女性**成员的独特传统，庆祝她们的**女性**力量和智慧。女性常常领导社区项目，受到平等尊重。这种进步的态度帮助王国在地区争端中保持**中立**，避免不必要的战争。

每年，王国都会在**高原**举行节日，人们从泉水中**取来**水，在大**盘子**上分享食物。这个活动象征着团结和对人们差异的**容忍**。人们唱着关于上方**天堂**和下方大地的歌曲，提醒大家与世界的联系。

王国的工匠还专门制作金属**合金**，结合了力量和美感。他们的工艺品远近闻名，带来财富和声誉。采矿和锻造的**沉重**工作与休闲和游戏的时刻相平衡。

在困难时期，社区会向邻近地区或自身的网络**乞求**帮助。但他们从未失去希望或对善良的承诺。对放弃的**拒绝**证明了他们的精神。

这个**王国**的故事提醒我们，文明不仅仅是权力或财富，而是我们建立的纽带、我们展示的尊重以及我们共同生活的恩典。它教导我们，即使在充满挑战的世界中，善良与合作也能创造持久的遗产。
