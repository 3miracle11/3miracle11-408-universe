# 基础词 Unit 29: The Prosperous Isle: A Tale of Turmoil and Triumph

中文标题：繁荣之岛：动荡与胜利的故事

## English Article

On a remote **isle** in the middle of the ocean, a small community thrived under the rule of a wise **dynasty**. The island was known for its **prosperity** and **prosperous** people, who lived in harmony with nature and each other. However, this peace was often tested by external **turmoil** and internal struggles.

The islanders were proud of their rich culture, which included **musical** traditions led by talented **musicians**. Every evening, the sound of a carefully tuned **tune** would fill the air, creating an atmosphere of **awe** and joy. The community's **tuition** system was unique, with experienced elders acting as **tutors** to the young, ensuring knowledge was passed down automatically and without fuss.

One day, a **cruise** ship arrived at the island's harbor, bringing visitors from a distant land. Among them was a **bully** who caused a **fuss** by being **rude** and **cruel** to the locals. He tried to **provoke** conflicts and even attempted to **crush** the islanders' spirit. But the people, united and strong, refused to be **dumb** or **mute** in the face of such behavior.

The island's economy relied heavily on the **bulk** export of **rubber** and **ivory**, products carefully harvested and **furnished** by skilled workers. They also produced **protein**-rich food from local sources, which helped the community stay healthy and resilient.

Despite the **awful** behavior of some visitors, the islanders continued to **protect** their home and traditions. They built a **tunnel** through the rocky terrain to improve access and used **automatic** systems powered by a **turbine** to generate electricity. This **automation** helped reduce manual labor and increased efficiency.

The island's **owner**, an elderly woman, was respected by all. She often spoke about the importance of **ownership** and responsibility, reminding everyone that they must care for their **surroundings** and the **province** they called home.

One evening at **dusk**, a strange **buzz** filled the air. The islanders gathered to witness a rare natural phenomenon: the **crystal** clear sky was illuminated by the shimmering **ozone** layer above. It was a moment of reflection and gratitude.

However, challenges remained. Some feared that superstition and **futile** protests against modernization could hinder progress. Yet, the community understood that **irrespective** of obstacles, they must continue to **fuse** tradition with innovation.

A prototype of a new **supermarket** was unveiled, designed to serve the islanders better and reduce waste. The project was a success, and the island's future looked brighter than ever.

In addition, the islanders learned to **tug** at the roots of their problems and **await** solutions patiently. They knew that to truly **prosper**, they must not only protect but also **isolate** harmful influences that could **ruin** their way of life. They used an **axe** to clear dead wood and **rub** the surfaces of their tools to keep them sharp. The islanders also treasured the soft **fur** of local animals, which they used to **furnish** their homes warmly.

**Furthermore**, they respected the **royal** traditions that shaped their governance, maintaining a balance around the **axis** of their community values. They found new **avenues** to **bypass** obstacles and chose to **dwell** peacefully, learning to **irrigate** their lands efficiently to support agriculture.

The islanders did not **mutter** complaints but instead sought guidance from a wise **tutor** who helped them **duplicate** successful strategies from afar. They understood they **owe** their survival to their unity and the **iron** will to overcome adversity, **owing** much to their ancestors' wisdom.

The story of this **dwarf** island teaches us that even small places can have great significance. By overcoming **rude** behavior, embracing **automation**, and valuing **ownership**, a community can thrive amidst adversity and **surrender** only to hope and determination.

This tale reminds us that whether faced with a **dump** of problems or the **crust** of hardship, unity and perseverance will always lead to **further** success. And as the islanders say, "But for our courage and care, we would not be here today."

## 中文译文

在海洋中央的一个偏远**小岛**上，一个小社区在一个睿智的**王朝**统治下繁荣发展。这个岛以其**繁荣**和**富裕**的人民而闻名，他们与自然和彼此和谐共处。然而，这种和平常常受到外部**动荡**和内部斗争的考验。

岛民们为他们丰富的文化感到自豪，其中包括由才华横溢的**音乐家**领导的**音乐**传统。每天晚上，精心调试的**曲调**的声音充满空气，营造出敬畏和欢乐的氛围。社区的**学费**体系独特，有经验的长者担任**导师**，确保知识自动且无纷扰地传承下去。

一天，一艘**游轮**抵达岛上的港口，带来了来自遥远土地的游客。其中有一个**欺凌者**因对当地人表现得**粗鲁**和**残忍**而引起了**骚动**。他试图**挑衅**冲突，甚至试图**粉碎**岛民的精神。但人民团结而坚强，拒绝在这种行为面前变得**愚笨**或**沉默**。

岛上的经济严重依赖大量出口**橡胶**和**象牙**，这些产品由熟练工人精心采集和**配备**。他们还生产富含**蛋白质**的本地食物，帮助社区保持健康和韧性。

尽管一些游客的行为**糟糕透顶**，岛民们继续**保护**他们的家园和传统。他们在岩石地形中修建了一条**隧道**以改善通行，并使用由**涡轮机**驱动的**自动**系统发电。这种**自动化**帮助减少了体力劳动，提高了效率。

岛上的**主人**是一位受人尊敬的老妇人。她经常谈论**所有权**和责任的重要性，提醒大家必须关心他们的**周围环境**和他们称之为家的**省份**。

一天傍晚，**黄昏**时分，空气中传来奇异的**嗡嗡声**。岛民们聚集起来，目睹罕见的自然现象：**水晶**般清澈的天空被上方闪烁的**臭氧**层照亮。这是一个反思和感恩的时刻。

然而，挑战依然存在。有些人担心迷信和对现代化的**徒劳**抗议可能阻碍进步。然而，社区明白，无论障碍如何，他们必须继续将传统与创新**融合**。

一个新的**超市**原型被揭幕，旨在更好地服务岛民并减少浪费。该项目取得了成功，岛屿的未来比以往任何时候都更加光明。

此外，岛民学会了**拉扯**问题的根源，耐心地**等待**解决方案。他们知道，要真正**繁荣**，不仅要保护，还要**隔离**可能**毁坏**他们生活方式的有害影响。他们用**斧头**砍伐枯木，并擦拭工具表面以保持锋利。岛民们还珍惜当地动物柔软的**皮毛**，用来温暖他们的家。

**此外**，他们尊重塑造他们治理的**皇家**传统，围绕社区价值的**轴心**保持平衡。他们找到了新的**途径**来**绕过**障碍，选择和平**居住**，学会有效地**灌溉**土地以支持农业。

岛民们不**咕哝**抱怨，而是寻求一位智慧**导师**的指导，帮助他们**复制**远方成功的策略。他们明白，他们的生存**归功于**团结和克服逆境的**铁**一般的意志，**因为**他们欠了祖先的智慧很多。

这个**矮小**岛屿的故事告诉我们，即使是小地方也能具有重大意义。通过克服**粗鲁**行为，拥抱**自动化**，并重视**所有权**，一个社区可以在逆境中繁荣发展，只向希望和决心**投降**。

这个故事提醒我们，无论面对多少**垃圾**问题或艰难的**外壳**，团结和坚持总会带来**进一步**的成功。正如岛民所说：“若非我们的勇气和关怀，我们今天不会在这里。”
