# 基础词 Unit 04: The Chairman’s Declaration: A Feat of Perseverance in the Realm of Pharmacy

中文标题：主席的宣言：药学领域中坚持不懈的壮举

## English Article

In the bustling **headquarters** of a renowned pharmaceutical company, the **chairman** prepared to make a historic **declaration**. The company had faced a **deficit** last quarter, and the **default** on some projects had caused a **quarrel** among the **members** of the board. Yet, the chairman was determined to **declare** a new vision that would **reap** rewards and restore the company's **glory**.

The meeting room, a large **chamber** filled with charts and graphs, was alive with tension. The **reader** on the screen displayed the **headline**: "Pharmacy Giant Faces Rapid Decline." But the chairman’s speech was a **feast** of hope and strategy. He emphasized the **feasibility** of their new product line, which included a revolutionary **pill** that could **mend** damaged tissues quickly.

Outside, the city was shrouded in **smog**, a **menace** to public health that the company aimed to combat. The chairman spoke of **membership** in a global alliance against pollution, highlighting their commitment to **charity** and environmental causes.

In a lighter moment, he joked about the **raw** enthusiasm of young scientists who often had a **rash** of ideas, some of which seemed **sly** or unconventional but sometimes led to breakthroughs. He mentioned a **pilot** project on a **slope** near the coast, where they tested a new drug delivery system that could **glide** smoothly through the bloodstream.

The chairman also addressed past **defects** in their production line, promising to **mend** them swiftly. He spoke of the **pillar** values of the company: integrity, innovation, and perseverance. Despite the **chaos** of the market and the **slump** in sales, he urged everyone to **persist** and not be discouraged by temporary setbacks.

He recounted a **feat** from the previous year when the team overcame a major **deficiency** in raw materials by sourcing alternatives quickly. This victory was as sweet as **champagne** and brought **glory** to the company.

The chairman concluded by urging the team to avoid the temptation to **smuggle** shortcuts or engage in unethical practices. He reminded them that every **deed** counted and that their **slogan** "Health for All" must remain true.

After the meeting, the members gathered for a casual **chat**. Some leaned on their **elbows**, discussing the **slot** for the next product launch, while others checked their **phone** for updates. A few joked about the **beard** of a senior scientist who looked like a **pilgrim** from history.

As the day ended, the chairman took a moment to reflect on the journey. From the **rear** of the building, he watched the **ray** of sunset piercing through the **chaos** of the city. Despite the challenges, the company was ready to face the future with smooth confidence and a clear vision.

This story of perseverance and innovation is a reminder that even in the face of adversity, a united team can overcome **menace** and **defeat**, turning raw potential into glorious achievement.

Moreover, the chairman emphasized the importance of **persuasion** and the ability to **persuade** stakeholders to support their vision. He noted that in the **realm** of pharmaceutical innovation, success depends not only on science but also on effective communication and leadership. The company’s **chancellor** of research was ready to lead the next **chapter** of development, ensuring no **defect** would cause the team to **degenerate** in their efforts.

He also warned against the **pest** of complacency and the dangers of unethical practices such as **rape** of natural resources or **smashing** competitors unfairly. The chairman urged the team to **defer** rash decisions and instead focus on **feasible** strategies that would build a strong **chain** of trust and progress.

In their logistics, the company planned to **eject** outdated methods and **glue** together a more efficient supply system, even considering the **quarantine** measures necessary during pandemics. The team was prepared to **bear** the **heat** of competition and the **gloomy** forecasts, confident that their **champion** spirit would prevail.

Finally, the chairman mentioned the environmental impact, including reducing **petrol** consumption and managing waste in urban **slums**. He encouraged everyone to **slip** past obstacles with agility and to treat every challenge like a **feather** in their cap, a symbol of resilience and achievement.

## 中文译文

在一家著名制药公司的繁忙总部，主席准备发表一项历史性的宣言。公司上个季度出现了赤字，一些项目的违约引发了董事会成员之间的争吵。然而，主席决心宣布一个新的愿景，将带来回报并恢复公司的荣耀。

会议室是一个充满图表和数据的大会议厅，气氛紧张。屏幕上的阅读器显示着头条新闻：“制药巨头面临快速衰退。”但主席的演讲充满了希望和战略。他强调了新产品线的可行性，其中包括一种能够快速修复受损组织的革命性药丸。

外面，城市笼罩在烟雾中，这是一种对公共健康的威胁，公司旨在对此进行抗击。主席谈到了加入全球反污染联盟，强调了他们对慈善和环境事业的承诺。

在轻松的时刻，他开玩笑说年轻科学家们充满了原始的热情，常常有一连串的想法，其中一些看似狡猾或非常规，但有时会带来突破。他提到了一个位于海岸附近坡地的试点项目，他们在那里测试了一种能够顺畅滑过血液的新药物输送系统。

主席还谈到了过去生产线上的缺陷，承诺迅速修复。他讲述了公司的支柱价值观：诚信、创新和坚持。尽管市场混乱，销售低迷，他敦促大家坚持不懈，不要被暂时的挫折打败。

他回忆起去年团队克服原材料严重不足的壮举，通过快速寻找替代品取得了胜利。这场胜利如同香槟般甜美，为公司带来了荣耀。

主席最后敦促团队避免走捷径或参与不道德行为。他提醒大家每一项行为都很重要，他们的口号“健康为全民”必须始终如一。

会议结束后，成员们聚在一起轻松聊天。有些人靠着肘部讨论下一次产品发布的时间段，其他人则查看手机更新。有几个人开玩笑说一位高级科学家的胡须看起来像历史上的朝圣者。

一天结束时，主席在建筑物后方静静地反思这段旅程。他看着夕阳的光线穿透城市的混乱。尽管面临挑战，公司已准备好以平稳的信心和清晰的愿景迎接未来。

这段坚持和创新的故事提醒我们，即使面对威胁和失败，团结的团队也能克服困难，将原始潜力转化为辉煌的成就。

此外，主席强调了说服力和说服利益相关者支持愿景的重要性。他指出，在制药创新领域，成功不仅依赖科学，还依赖有效的沟通和领导力。公司的研究总监准备领导下一章节的发展，确保没有缺陷会导致团队努力的退化。

他还警告了自满的害虫以及诸如掠夺自然资源或不公平打击竞争对手等不道德行为的危险。主席敦促团队推迟仓促决策，专注于可行的策略，建立起信任和进步的坚固链条。

在物流方面，公司计划淘汰过时的方法，粘合更高效的供应系统，甚至考虑疫情期间必要的隔离措施。团队准备承受竞争的压力和阴郁的预测，自信他们的冠军精神将会胜出。

最后，主席提到了环境影响，包括减少汽油消耗和管理城市贫民窟的废弃物。他鼓励大家灵活应对障碍，把每一个挑战都视为帽子上的羽毛，象征着韧性和成就。
