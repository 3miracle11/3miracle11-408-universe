# 基础词 Unit 24: The Courageous Journey of a Tribal Dragon

中文标题：勇敢的部落龙之旅

## English Article

In a small **tribe** nestled between the mountains and the sea, a **dragon** was more than a myth—it was a symbol of **courage** and strength. The villagers believed that the dragon’s **fragrant** breath could bring life to the barren lands and that its **bright** scales shone like a **mosaic** of precious stones.

One day, a **storm** struck the village, causing a **wound** in the earth that threatened to **drain** the life from their **foundation**. The elders decided to **bring** together a **couple** of brave souls to seek the dragon’s help. Among them was a young man named Taro, known for his **drastic** decisions and fearless heart.

As they prepared to **exit** the village, a **stranger** appeared at the **forum**, offering a **coupon** that promised safe passage through the dangerous **strand** of cliffs nearby. Though the offer seemed like a **trick**, Taro chose to **wrap** the coupon carefully in his bag, hoping it would be useful.

The journey was tough. They had to **drag** heavy supplies, **draw** maps, and avoid the **foul** winds that blew down from the mountains. At one point, Taro had to **stoop** low to avoid falling debris during a rockslide. The group faced many **trials**, including a **fracture** in the path that forced them to find a new route.

Along the way, they crossed a **bridge** over a rushing **stream** where Taro took a deep **breath** and felt the cool **breeze** refresh his spirit. They passed a **fountain** where the water was so clear it reflected the **triangle** of peaks above. Despite the hardships, the **worldwide** tales of the dragon kept their hopes alive.

One night, Taro had a strange dream of a **drawer** filled with ancient scrolls and a **coupon** that could grant wishes. He woke up feeling a **breath** of inspiration and decided to **foster** hope among his companions. They moved **forward**, even when the path seemed **downward** and the situation grew **worse**.

Finally, they reached the dragon’s lair, a place filled with **fragile** crystals that glittered like stars. The dragon, a **brilliant** creature with scales that seemed to **streamline** its massive body, greeted them. Taro offered the **tribute** of the village’s hopes and the **coupon** from the stranger.

The dragon, recognizing their **mortal** courage, agreed to help. It breathed a **fragrant** mist over the land, healing the **wound** and restoring the **foundation**. The villagers celebrated with a **drawing** of the dragon on a large **straw** tapestry, symbolizing their unity.

On their way back **downtown**, the group encountered a **coward** who tried to **bribe** them to abandon their quest. But Taro’s team stood firm, knowing that even a small **trifle** of doubt could cause a **wreck**. They avoided the **drawback** of fear and pressed on.

During a brief **trip** through a nearby meadow, they noticed a **cow** grazing calmly, a peaceful contrast to their perilous journey. Taro took a moment to **breathe** deeply, feeling the fresh air fill his lungs. He adjusted the **strap** of his pack and gave his cloak a quick **trim** to stay comfortable on the **course** ahead.

In the end, the journey was a **trial** of spirit and strength. The **couple** of heroes returned as legends, their story told at every **exhibit** and **forum**. The dragon’s legacy lived **worldwide**, inspiring all who heard it to face their fears and embrace the unknown.

This tale reminds us that even when life seems **down**, and the path is **strange**, with **courage** and determination, we can overcome any obstacle and find our way home.

Along the way, they found a small **fragment** of ancient pottery, a **fraction** of a once grand artifact, which they carefully preserved. At night, when exhaustion threatened to make them **doze**, they stayed vigilant, knowing that exile from their homes was a fate worse than any hardship. The story of the dragon and its **triple** blessings continues to **exile** fear and **worry** from the hearts of those who believe, much like a **worm** transforming the earth beneath them.

## 中文译文

在一个坐落于群山与大海之间的小**部落**里，**龙**不仅仅是神话——它是**勇气**和力量的象征。村民们相信，龙那**芳香**的气息能为贫瘠的土地带来生命，而它那**明亮**的鳞片如同一幅珍贵宝石的**马赛克**。

有一天，一场**风暴**袭击了村庄，造成了地面上的一道**伤口**，威胁着他们的**基础**生命。长老们决定召集一**对**勇敢的人，去寻求龙的帮助。其中有一个名叫太郎的年轻人，以其**激烈**的决定和无畏的心闻名。

当他们准备**离开**村庄时，一个**陌生人**出现在**论坛**上，递给他们一张承诺安全通过附近危险**海滨**悬崖的**优惠券**。虽然这个提议看起来像个**诡计**，但太郎还是小心地将优惠券**包裹**在包里，希望它能派上用场。

旅程十分艰难。他们必须**拖**着沉重的补给，**绘制**地图，避开从山上吹下的**恶劣**风。在一次山体滑坡中，太郎不得不**弯腰**躲避落石。团队经历了许多**考验**，包括路径上的**骨折**，迫使他们寻找新路线。

途中，他们经过一座横跨湍急**溪流**的**桥梁**，太郎深吸一口气，感受到凉爽的**微风**让他的精神焕发。他们经过一处**喷泉**，水清澈见底，映照出上方三角形的山峰。尽管困难重重，龙的**全球**传说依然让他们充满希望。

一天晚上，太郎做了一个奇怪的梦，梦见一个装满古卷轴的**抽屉**和一张能实现愿望的**优惠券**。他醒来时感受到一股灵感的**气息**，决定在同伴中**培养**希望。他们继续**前进**，即使路途显得**向下**，情况变得更**糟糕**。

最终，他们到达了龙的巢穴，那里布满了如星星般闪烁的**脆弱**水晶。龙是一只**辉煌**的生物，鳞片似乎能使它的庞大身体**流线型**。太郎献上了村庄的希望和那个陌生人的**贡品**——优惠券。

龙认可了他们作为**凡人**的勇气，答应帮助他们。它吐出一阵**芳香**的雾气，治愈了土地上的**伤口**，恢复了**基础**。村民们用一幅巨大的**稻草**挂毯绘制了龙的图画，象征他们的团结。

回到**市区**的路上，团队遇到了一个**懦夫**，试图用贿赂让他们放弃任务。但太郎的团队坚定不移，知道即使一丝**琐事**的怀疑也可能导致**毁坏**。他们避免了恐惧的**缺点**，继续前行。

在一次穿越附近草地的短暂**旅行**中，他们看到一头安静吃草的**奶牛**，与他们危险的旅程形成鲜明对比。太郎深深地**呼吸**新鲜空气，调整了背包的**带子**，并快速**修剪**了他的斗篷，以便在接下来的**课程**中保持舒适。

最终，这次旅程是对精神和力量的**考验**。这对英雄回来了，成为传奇，他们的故事在每个**展览**和**论坛**上讲述。龙的遗产传遍**全球**，激励所有听到它的人面对恐惧，拥抱未知。

途中，他们发现了一块古陶器的**碎片**，是曾经宏伟文物的一小**部分**，他们小心保存。夜晚，当疲惫威胁让他们打盹时，他们保持警惕，知道被放逐是比任何困难都更糟的命运。龙和它的**三重**祝福的故事继续将恐惧和**忧虑**驱逐出信仰者的心中，就像**蠕虫**改变着他们脚下的土地一样。
