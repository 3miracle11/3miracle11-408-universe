# 基础词 Unit 20: The Remarkable Legacy of the Greenhouse Angel

中文标题：温室天使的非凡遗产

## English Article

In a small coastal town, nestled between the green hills and the roaring tide, there was a remarkable story that tied the community together like a tight cluster of stars in the night sky. This story was about an angel who had left a lasting legacy that enriched the lives of innumerable residents.

The tale began many years ago, on a weekday when the sky was unusually cloudy and the air felt heavy with the threat of pollution. The town was facing a desperate situation: the nearby river, once a pool of clear water, was now contaminated, and the local engineer was struggling to find a solution. The engine that powered the water purification system was old and failing, and the community was on the brink of despair.

One day, an antique lens was discovered in the attic of the town’s oldest house. It was part of an old antenna system, but its unique design gave the engineer an idea. Inspired, he began to enrich the water purification process by incorporating this lens, which helped filter out pollutants more effectively. The results were better than anyone had hoped.

As the water quality improved, so did the town’s spirit. People began to greet each other with renewed hope, and the once desolate streets filled with laughter and life. The weekly market thrived, and children played freely, their clothing no longer stained by the dirty water. Even the local fireman noticed a change; the air was cleaner, and the risk of fire caused by pollution-related hazards diminished.

However, the story’s true hero was not the engineer or the fireman, but a mysterious figure known only as the Greenhouse Angel. This person was said to have appeared during the town’s darkest hours, offering guidance and encouragement. Some believed the Angel was an ancestor watching over the town, while others thought it was a guardian spirit.

The Angel’s presence was marked by small but meaningful gestures: a remark left on a bulletin board encouraging people to beware of careless waste disposal, a lecture at the local school about the dangers of pollution, and an announcement urging residents to take part in a cleanup event. These actions, though simple, had a profound impact.

One memorable anecdote involved a young girl who found an injured bird near the cloudy riverbank. She carefully clutched it in her hands and took it to the Greenhouse, a community center dedicated to environmental education. There, volunteers worked tirelessly to nurse the bird back to health, symbolizing the town’s commitment to protecting its natural heritage.

The Greenhouse itself was a marvel. Constructed with sustainable materials and powered by solar energy, it served as a hub for learning and activism. Inside, visitors could see exhibits on the town’s history, the science behind pollution control, and the importance of preserving the environment for future generations.

The Angel’s influence extended beyond environmental issues. The community learned to tie their fates together, understanding that their destiny was intertwined with the health of their surroundings. They became more mindful of their actions, avoiding greedy exploitation of resources and instead choosing to lend a hand to neighbors in need.

Despite the challenges, the town’s spirit remained tight and resilient. The people knew that even when faced with final obstacles, they could overcome them by working and caring for each other. The Greenhouse Angel’s story was a legacy of hope, reminding everyone that even in the face of destruction and despair, there is always a way forward.

On the town’s annual anniversary celebration, residents gathered to honor the Angel’s memory. They shared stories, sang songs, and planted trees along the riverbank to prevent further weed growth and soil erosion. These trees would one day form a natural barrier against the polar winds that swept through the region.

The community also took part in an inquiry into the causes of past pollution, determined to learn from mistakes. They invited experts to give lectures on sustainable practices and encouraged everyone to participate in weekly cleanups. The town’s transformation was so profound that it became a well-known example of environmental recovery.

One day, a visitor asked the mayor about the secret to their success. The mayor smiled and said, "It’s simple: we never lost sight of what lies beyond the immediate problems. We understood that every small action counts, and we always kept an inner belief in a better future."

The Greenhouse Angel’s story is a testament to the power of community, resilience, and hope. It reminds us all to bewilder the forces of destruction with kindness and determination, to enquire deeply into the causes of our problems, and to work tirelessly to create a world where every living thing can thrive.

In the end, the town’s journey was not just about cleaning a river or building a greenhouse; it was about the human spirit’s ability to finish what it starts, to clothe itself in courage, and to face every challenge with an open heart. This legacy, like the stars in the sky, will continue to shine brightly for generations to come.

## 中文译文

在一个小海滨小镇，坐落在翠绿的山丘和咆哮的潮水之间，有一个非凡的故事将社区紧密联系在一起，就像夜空中紧密相连的星团一样。这个故事讲述了一位天使，她留下了丰富的遗产，丰富了无数居民的生活。

故事始于许多年前的一个平日，当时天空异常多云，空气中弥漫着污染的威胁。小镇正面临着绝望的局面：附近的河流曾经是清澈的水池，如今却被污染，当地的工程师正在努力寻找解决方案。为水净化系统提供动力的引擎陈旧且故障频发，社区濒临绝望的边缘。

一天，镇上最古老的房子阁楼里发现了一枚古董镜头。它是旧天线系统的一部分，但其独特的设计给工程师带来了灵感。受此启发，他开始通过结合这枚镜头来丰富水净化过程，这帮助更有效地过滤污染物。结果比任何人预期的都要好。

随着水质的改善，镇上的精神也随之振奋。人们开始以焕然一新的希望互相问候，曾经荒凉的街道充满了欢笑和生机。每周市场繁荣起来，孩子们自由地玩耍，他们的衣服不再被肮脏的水弄脏。甚至当地的消防员也注意到变化；空气更清新，因污染引发的火灾风险减少了。

然而，故事的真正英雄不是工程师或消防员，而是一位神秘人物，被称为温室天使。据说这位天使在镇上最黑暗的时刻出现，提供指导和鼓励。有些人相信天使是守护小镇的祖先，也有人认为她是守护神灵。

天使的存在通过一些小而有意义的举动体现出来：在公告板上留下提醒人们注意随意丢弃垃圾的警告，在当地学校举办关于污染危害的讲座，以及发布号召居民参与清理活动的公告。这些简单的行动产生了深远的影响。

一个难忘的轶事是关于一个小女孩，她在多云的河岸边发现了一只受伤的鸟。她小心翼翼地把它捧在手中，带到温室——一个致力于环境教育的社区中心。志愿者们不知疲倦地照顾这只鸟，象征着小镇保护自然遗产的承诺。

温室本身就是一个奇迹。它采用可持续材料建造，利用太阳能供电，成为学习和环保活动的中心。参观者可以看到有关小镇历史、污染控制科学以及保护环境重要性的展览。

天使的影响不仅限于环境问题。社区学会了将命运紧密联系在一起，明白他们的未来与环境健康息息相关。他们更加注意自己的行为，避免贪婪地开发资源，而是选择帮助有需要的邻居。

尽管面临挑战，小镇的精神依然紧密而坚韧。人们知道，即使面对最终的障碍，也能通过合作和关爱彼此克服。温室天使的故事是一份希望的遗产，提醒大家即使面对毁灭和绝望，总有前进的道路。

在小镇的周年庆典上，居民们聚集在一起纪念天使。他们分享故事，唱歌，并在河岸种植树木，以防止杂草生长和水土流失。这些树木将来会形成一道天然屏障，抵御吹过该地区的极地风。

社区还参与了对过去污染原因的调查，决心从错误中吸取教训。他们邀请专家举办关于可持续实践的讲座，鼓励每个人参加每周的清理活动。小镇的转变如此深刻，成为环境恢复的著名典范。

一天，一位访客问市长他们成功的秘诀。市长微笑着说：“很简单：我们从未忽视眼前问题之外的事物。我们明白每一个小行动都很重要，并始终怀有内心对美好未来的信念。”

温室天使的故事证明了社区、韧性和希望的力量。它提醒我们用善良和决心迷惑毁灭的力量，深入探究问题的根源，并不懈努力创造一个所有生命都能繁荣的世界。

最终，小镇的旅程不仅仅是清理河流或建造温室；它是人类精神完成所开始之事的能力，是勇气的外衣，是以开放的心态面对每一个挑战。这份遗产，如同天空中的星星，将继续为后代闪耀光芒。
