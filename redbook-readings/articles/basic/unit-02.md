# 基础词 Unit 02: A Journey Through Childhood Memories and Modern Challenges

中文标题：穿越童年记忆与现代挑战的旅程

## English Article

In the **shabby** old town where I grew up, the **chief** memory that stands out is the **delicious** aroma of home-cooked meals. Despite the **shallow** economic conditions, the **texture** of life was rich with stories and laughter. As a child, I would often **grin** from ear to ear when I walked down the **aisle** of the local market, where vendors sold everything from **textile** fabrics to fresh fruits.

One summer, my family decided to **emigrate** to a new **territory** across the sea. The thought of leaving behind my **childhood** friends filled me with both **delight** and **terror**. I was **terrified** at the idea of starting over but also **alert** to the opportunities that awaited. At the airport, the **air-conditioning** was a relief from the **chill** of the early morning, and I watched the **aircraft** take off, feeling a mix of excitement and sadness.

The journey was long, and I tried to **grind** my teeth against the anxiety. I could feel my hands **shake** as we passed through customs, where an **official** officer checked our documents. The **electrical** systems in the new city were impressive, with **elevators** and **electricians** working to keep everything running smoothly.

In school, I was often **embarrassed** by my accent but learned to **align** myself with new friends who were **alike** in their experiences. We formed a **chorus** club, and our **eloquent** performances brought us closer. Sometimes, I would **chew** on my pencil nervously before a big test, hoping not to **cheat** but to do my best.

Life wasn’t always easy. There were moments of **grief** when I missed my family back home. I would **grieve** quietly, feeling a **chronic** ache in my heart. Yet, the community around me was supportive, and I learned the importance of **empathy**. We shared stories of hardship and hope, creating an **empire** of friendship.

One day, a **delegate** from the local government visited our school to **elect** new student leaders. The process was fair, and the chosen students were **eligible** and enthusiastic. I felt proud to be part of such an **official** event.

At home, my parents worked hard, sometimes dealing with **waste** and **shame** when things didn’t go as planned. But they never gave up. They taught me to **wash** away doubts and to **shave** off fears, encouraging me to face challenges head-on.

Technology fascinated me. I learned about **algorithms** and how they **emit** signals to make devices smarter. The **shaft** of a fan in our room rotated smoothly, powered by **electric** currents. I even helped fix a broken **shampoo** dispenser once, using skills I picked up from an **electrician**.

Despite the hardships, life was **alive** with possibilities. I remember the **chunk** of ice I held on a hot day, feeling the cold **chill** spread through my fingers. Watching the sunset from the **office** window, I realized how far I had come.

Sometimes, I would **pinch** myself to ensure it wasn’t a dream. The **fee** for education was high, but worth every penny. I learned to **delete** negative thoughts and focus on the positive. Even when things seemed **feeble**, I found strength in my memories and the people around me.

Now, as I look back, I understand that every **shatter** of fear and every **shake** of uncertainty shaped who I am. The **terrific** journey from a **shabby** town to a thriving city taught me resilience and hope. And through it all, the lessons of kindness, courage, and perseverance remain my guiding light.

I also remember the **terrible** moments that would **embarrass** me deeply, but these only helped me grow. At times, I was **terrified** to **embark** on new adventures, yet I always moved **ahead** with determination. I learned to **shed** old fears and to **elevate** my spirit, much like riding an **elevator** to new heights. There were times I would clutch my **chest** in anxiety, but I found ways to calm myself and keep going. Sometimes, I would **pin** my hopes on small successes, knowing that every step forward mattered. These **empirical** experiences shaped my understanding of the world and myself, turning an **empty** feeling of doubt into confidence and strength.

## 中文译文

生活并不总是容易。有时我会感到**悲痛**，想念远方的家人。我会默默地**悲伤**，心中有一种**慢性**的痛楚。然而，周围的社区很支持我，我学会了**同理心**。我们分享艰难与希望的故事，建立了一个友谊的**帝国**。

一天，当地政府的一名**代表**来学校**选举**新的学生领导。过程公平，选出的学生都符合资格且充满热情。我为能参与这样一个**官方**活动感到自豪。

在家里，父母努力工作，有时遇到**浪费**和**羞耻**，事情不顺利时也不放弃。他们教我洗去疑虑，剃除恐惧，鼓励我勇敢面对挑战。

尽管困难重重，生活依然充满**活力**。我记得炎热天握着一块冰块，感受冰凉的**寒意**从指尖蔓延。透过**办公室**的窗户看夕阳，我意识到自己走了多远。

有时，我会**掐**自己，确认这不是梦。教育的**费用**很高，但每一分钱都值得。我学会了**删除**消极想法，专注于积极面。即使当一切看似**虚弱**，我也从记忆和周围的人中找到力量。

现在回望过去，我明白每一次恐惧的**破碎**和每一次不确定的**颤抖**都塑造了今天的我。从一个**破旧**小镇到繁荣城市的**极好的**旅程教会了我坚韧和希望。在这一切中，善良、勇气和坚持的教训始终是我前行的指路明灯。
